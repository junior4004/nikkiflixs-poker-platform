Security & Fair Play

How {{CASINO_NAME}} protects your game, your account, and your coins.

RNG

Certified-grade random shuffling

Every deck is shuffled server-side using a cryptographically secure random number generator (the operating system's CSPRNG — the same class of randomness banks use for encryption keys), not the predictable Math.random() found in browser games. Shuffles happen on our servers, never on your device, so no player can see or influence the deck.

Operators pursuing real-money licensure can take our RNG through independent certification (iTech Labs, GLI, or BMM) — the code is written with a single, auditable randomness choke point to make that straightforward.

SERVER-AUTHORITATIVE

The server is the referee

All game logic — dealing, betting rounds, pot calculation, side pots — runs on our servers. Your app only displays the game state and sends your actions. There is no way to peek at other players' cards, alter chip counts, or replay hands from the client side.

ANTI-COLLUSION

Fair-play monitoring

  • Player connections are tracked per table — shared-IP seating is automatically flagged for review.
  • Showdown patterns are analyzed for suspicious coordination between accounts.
  • Administrators can warn, suspend, or ban accounts, and every action is logged.
ACCOUNT SECURITY

Your account, locked down

  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes — never in plain text, never reversible.
  • Optional two-factor authentication (authenticator app) for players and administrators.
  • All traffic is encrypted with TLS/HTTPS with HSTS enforcement.
  • Sessions are signed tokens that expire; banned accounts are rejected at every layer.
FUNDS

Coin integrity

Coin balances live server-side, not on your device. Every coin movement — purchases, bonuses, adjustments, redemptions — is written to an append-only transaction ledger with timestamps. Gold Coins have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes.

RESPONSIBLE PLAY

Play responsibly

This is a social gaming platform. If gambling stops being fun, help is available 24/7 at 1-800-GAMBLER (US). You must be 18 or older to play (19+ in AL/NE, 21+ in AZ and where required by law).
